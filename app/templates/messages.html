Good to know

  • Only the first text to a lead is pre-filled. Once anyone on your team has texted them, the button opens blank so you don't repeat yourself.
  • Everyone on your team gets the same wording, signed with their own name.
  • The automatic acknowledgement a lead gets the moment their form arrives, and appointment confirmations, are set up by {{ settings.agency_name }}. Ask them if you'd like those changed too.
